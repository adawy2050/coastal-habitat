# Data and methods

## Heatwave atlas

The atlas maps heatwave metrics at coastal habitat locations in three study areas: the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden, the Arabian Gulf, and East Asia and the western Pacific. Habitat locations are display and analysis points; the temperature values come from the underlying sea-surface-temperature grid.

The atlas includes GHRSST MUR at 0.01° for 2003–2025 and NOAA OISST at 0.25° for 1982–2025. MHW histories use the saved study detections and the corresponding reference periods: 2003–2022 for MUR and 1982–2011 for OISST. Events are based on seasonally varying 90th-percentile thresholds and a five-day minimum duration. The atlas reports annual days, frequency, mean and maximum intensity, cumulative intensity and mean duration.

The point view combines the selected location's history with daily or monthly temperature, climate-index records and the seasonal estimate where available. Climate-index associations are statistical relationships; they do not establish cause. Pixel-level estimates use the native temperature-cell history, so multiple habitat points can share a source cell.

The seasonal outlook is tested against earlier seasons and shown with its historical baseline. It is a probability estimate, not a confirmed warning. Historical heatwave records end in 2025; partial 2026 observations are shown separately when available.

## Kelp portal

The kelp viewer presents saved historical and model results for *Saccharina longissima* and *S. japonica*. The Longissima series includes historical and future scenarios; the Japonica series is historical only. Point and polygon summaries retain their reported biomass and erosion units. Nearby photographs provide geographic context and are not observations of the selected station.
