# Kelp portal

The kelp digital twin presents saved results for two species of *Saccharina*.

- *S. longissima*: historical results for 1996–2020 and two future scenarios for 2021–2100.
- *S. japonica*: historical results for 1991–2020; no future model run is included.

The map includes model stations and mapped habitat areas. Point reports show biomass history, local heatwave history and factors that may help interpret unusually low values. Polygon and grid views show the available biomass and erosion summaries. These are model and mapped results, not real-time measurements.

The portal uses saved model outputs and does not extend the model beyond its supplied years. Habitat photographs, papers and acknowledgements retain their source credits in the portal.
