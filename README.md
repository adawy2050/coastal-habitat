# Coastal habitat observatory

Explore marine heatwaves across coastal habitats in the Red Sea, Arabian Gulf, East Asia and the western Pacific, or visit the kelp digital twin.

- [Open the atlas](https://adawy2050.github.io/coastal-habitat/heatwaves.html)
- [Open the kelp portal](https://adawy2050.github.io/coastal-habitat/kelp/)

## Related paper

Eladawy, A., Shaltout, M. Habitat-scale marine heatwave exposure in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden: long-term warming, seasonal change and climate associations. *Theor Appl Climatol* **157**, 817 (2026). [https://doi.org/10.1007/s00704-026-06702-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00704-026-06702-0)

The atlas includes habitat-cell heatwave histories, climate-index charts, seasonal outlooks and downloadable point reports. See [Data and methods](DATA_METHODS.md) for a short description of the products and analysis.
